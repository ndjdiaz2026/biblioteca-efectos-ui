Your Day On The Water

From the moment you step on board to the final golden hour toast, every detail is orchestrated for joy.

1

The Welcome

Step onto the pristine deck. Handed a signature welcome cocktail, you immediately feel the Caribbean rhythm as our crew welcomes your group like family.

2

Coastal Cruising

Feel the breeze as we sail along the stunning Bavaro coastline. The music sets the mood—your playlist, your vibe. Sunbathe on the nets or dance under the canopy.

3

The Natural Pool

We drop anchor in waist-deep, crystal-clear turquoise waters. Our crew brings the floating bar to you. Swim, toast, and capture the ultimate group photos.

4

The Culinary & Celebration Peak

Back on board, the Floating Kitchen serves up your freshly prepared meal. As the energy peaks, we turn up the music for the ultimate ocean celebration.
